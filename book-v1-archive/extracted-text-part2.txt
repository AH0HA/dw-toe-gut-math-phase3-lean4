Technical Glossary

Aetheric refractive index (δ): A small dimensionless parameter used in the
framework to represent departure from perfect superfluid coherence; it
functions as the main bridge between ideal geometry and dissipative physics.

Breadth: The extent of coherent coupling across a system — how many
standing waves or subsystems are integrated into one organized field.

Charge metabolism: The proposed conversion of normal-fluid entropy back
into superfluid order at or around the still point; this is Axiom 6’s negentropic
engine.

before phase-locking fails and fragmentation begins.

Concentration: The local intensity of still-point realization, modeled by how
closely a system approaches δ → 0.

D₅: The five-dimensional sublattice used as the first major projection step
from E₈ in the book’s geometric chain.

Differentiation: The number of distinct, specialized subsystems active within
one standing wave.

deepest geometric source structure.

H₃: The icosahedral Coxeter symmetry used to organize the second major step
in the projection from E₈ toward three-dimensional form.

On-ladder / off-ladder: Shorthand for whether interacting modes remain on the
φ-closed spectral ladder (constructive) or generate non-closing interference
products (destructive).

Phase conjugation: Complex conjugation of the order parameter, reversing
velocity fields and defining the still-point condition Ψ = Ψ*.

φ-ladder: A geometric frequency ladder in which adjacent ratios are set by the
golden ratio φ; used throughout the book as the preferred closure structure for
stable interference.
Standing wave: A self-organized, spatially persistent mode structure; in this
framework, particles, bodies, and larger systems are treated as standing waves
of different scales.

Still point: A phase-conjugate fixed point where velocity vanishes, symmetry
is highest, and the framework places the deepest analogue of consciousness.
