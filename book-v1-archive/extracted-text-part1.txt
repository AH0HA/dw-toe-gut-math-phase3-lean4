distributed along the spinal column, each one a specialized mode-processing
node handling a distinct band of the biological φ-ladder.
A human has seven or more major processing centers along the spinal axis,
plus sub-centers, plus the recursive cortical hierarchy with its own internal
specialization (prefrontal, temporal, parietal, occipital — each handling
different cognitive functions). Dozens of distinct processing subsystems
operating simultaneously. This extreme differentiation is unique in the known
biological hierarchy.

processing channels.

valid experiential modes. What changes across the ladder is the character of
the game, not the value of the player.

of feeling, thought, conflict, meaning, and choice that no simpler or more
integrated mode reproduces in quite the same way.
