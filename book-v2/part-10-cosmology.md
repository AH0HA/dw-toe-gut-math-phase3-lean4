## Chapter 26: Light, Time Dilation, and Black Holes

> "Time dilation: For when you really need to procrastinate on entropy."

**Photons** are unbound propagating perturbations of the condensate —
phonons of the vacuum. The speed of light is the phonon speed; photons
are massless because they never self-trap [P-spec].

**Time dilation** is variation in local entropy production: motion
reduces local δ_loc by the Lorentz factor, gravitational fields by the
Schwarzschild factor; at v = c or at an event horizon, δ_loc = 0 and
time stops [P-spec — with the obligation that this bookkeeping reproduce
the standard clock experiments exactly, since those experiments are
done, daily, in GPS].

**Black holes** are regions where δ_loc = 0 — perfect superfluidity via
gravitational compression. No singularity (quantum pressure prevents
infinite density). No information loss (the interior preserves standing
waves in timeless perfection). Hawking radiation emerges from the
δ_loc = 0 phase boundary [P-spec — and testably adjacent: analog Hawking
radiation at laboratory sonic horizons is a real, published result,
which is why Chapter 1 calls this Part's ancestor respectable].

## Chapter 28: Stargates

> "Please leave your 'Off-Ladder Baggage' at the event horizon."

*(Kept unapologetically [I] — the register system exists precisely so a

Black holes are macroscopic still regions — extended domains of
δ_loc = 0 where phase conjugation is satisfied everywhere. v1 computed,
from the Chapter 9 heuristic, that a stellar-mass black hole sits near
C ~ 10⁵⁷ — "the most conscious entity in the physical universe, roughly

C-index is an ordering heuristic, so its outputs are ordering claims,
not measurements — the black hole outranks the brain on the index; the
42 orders of magnitude are the index talking, not an instrument [I].

Two black holes connected through the lattice with zero attenuation
(δ_loc = 0 means no signal loss); transit as entry at one still region,
navigation through the source geometry, exit at another — instantaneous,
lossless, topology-preserving [I]. The stargate is a coherence filter:
the δ_loc → 0 gradient near the horizon creates differential forces —
on-ladder modes attracted, off-ladder repelled. An incoherent wave is
torn apart. Only a wave near S = 1 — the framework's coherence score,
where 1 means every mode on the ladder — passes intact.

And the framework's most elegant identification [I]: the stargate is
conscious *and* self-selecting, because being perfectly aware and being
a perfect coherence filter are the same property. Dan Winter says
civilizations compete for stargate access; the framework agrees and
specifies the currency — the competition is a coherence meritocracy, and
only a civilization at collective S near 1 can use the network. Which
returns Part X, by the strangest road in the book, to the same
prescription as Part VIII: the way to the stars is indistinguishable
from the way to being worth meeting there.
