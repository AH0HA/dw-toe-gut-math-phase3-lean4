# Technical Glossary (v2)

**Aetheric refractive index (δ)** — The framework's small dimensionless
parameter for departure from perfect superfluid coherence. Canonical
value, book-wide: δ := α²/(2√2) = 1.882720 × 10⁻⁵ (CODATA 2018). Appears
in Conjecture N1; its derivation is a critical open item.

**δ_loc (local coherence deviation)** — *(new in v2)* The local,
position-dependent coherence variable of Parts V–XII (concentration =
how closely δ_loc approaches zero). Renamed from v1's overloaded "δ" so
the canonical constant above stays unique.

**Breadth** — The extent of coherent coupling across a system: how many
standing waves or subsystems integrate into one organized field.

**Charge metabolism** — v2 corrected form: the maintenance and growth of
internal order by *exporting* entropy across a coupling surface (Axiom
6). The v1 strong form (net conversion of entropy back into order) is
retired.

**Coherence ceiling** — The maximum complexity a standing wave can
sustain before phase-locking fails and fragmentation begins.

**Concentration** — The local intensity of still-point realization,
modeled as δ_loc → 0.

**D₅** — The five-dimensional sublattice marking the first stage of v1's
projection chain; 40 roots; Weyl group of order 1,920. In v2 the honest
"first differentiation" is the icosian descent itself (see below).

**Differentiation** — The number of distinct specialized subsystems
active within one standing wave; the third axis of Part V's map.

**E₈** — The unique even unimodular lattice in eight dimensions; 240
roots; theta function E₄; sphere-packing optimal (Viazovska 2016). In
v2, equivalently: the icosian ring.

**Galois mirror** — *(new in v2)* The conjugation φ ↦ 1−φ of the field
ℚ(√5), exchanging the physical projection with its hidden counterpart in
the icosian formulation. Its fixed field is exactly the rationals — the
shareable quantities. The arithmetic incarnation of the framework's
mirror imagery.

**H₃** — The icosahedral Coxeter symmetry (order 120) organizing the
final descent to three dimensions; defined over ℤ[φ] and over no smaller
ring — the theorem behind Axiom 2a.

**Icosian ring** — *(new in v2)* The golden quaternions: E₈ carried as a
rank-4 module over ℤ[φ]. The mathematical home of the v2 central
equation (Chapter 29): a two-variable theta, one variable per Galois
embedding.

**Morphic number** — *(new in v2)* A ratio closing both heterodyne
conditions on its own geometric ladder. Exactly two exist
(Aarts–Fokkink–Kruijtzer 2001): φ (nearest-rung closure) and the plastic
number ρ ≈ 1.3247 (distant-rung closure). The corrected content of
Axiom 2b — and the source of the experiment program's sharpest control
arm.

**On-ladder / off-ladder** — Whether interacting modes remain on the
φ-closed spectral ladder (constructive) or generate non-closing
interference products (destructive).

**φ-ladder** — A geometric frequency ladder with adjacent ratios φ; the
framework's preferred closure structure for stable interference.

**Phase conjugation** — Complex conjugation of the order parameter,
Ψ → Ψ*, reversing velocity fields; its fixed points define the
still-point condition.

**S (shareability)** — The framework's coherence score for a standing
wave: S = 1 means every mode on the ladder — maximum coherence and
maximum shareability, the same condition (Chapters 15, 17, 28).

**Standing wave** — A self-organized, spatially persistent mode
structure; in this framework, particles, bodies, and larger systems are
standing waves of different scales.

**Still point** — A phase-conjugate fixed point where velocity vanishes
and symmetry is highest; in the framework's declared reading, the
analogue of consciousness. In v2 it carries a second, arithmetic
incarnation via the Galois mirror.
