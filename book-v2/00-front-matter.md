## Reader's Caution / Framework Status (v2)

This book is a mathematical audit, correction, and extension of Dan
Winter's Super Golden Fractal Theory of Everything. Its claims are
tagged, sentence by sentence where necessary, in three registers:

- **[M] Mathematics** — proven, or an open conjecture labeled as such.
- **[P] Physics** — falsifiable postulates with named falsifiers.
- **[I] Interpretation** — declared readings the mathematics is
  compatible with but does not prove.

The status ledger, in one paragraph: One zero-parameter numerical
observation stands (the mass-ratio formula, matching measurement to 2.3
parts per billion — an unexplained coincidence until a mechanism
predicts it, and labeled so). One v1 formula is retired (the δ⁸ gravity
bridge — it failed its own accuracy claim under audit; Chapter 5 now
narrates its retirement as a working example of the method). One v1
theorem-claim is corrected (φ's uniqueness — it shares closure with
exactly one sibling, the plastic number, and is unique only at the
nearest rungs). The central conjecture is replaced (Chapter 29). The
practical coherence protocol (Chapter 34) is presented as a disciplined
practice model, its first phase resting on mainstream-validated
physiology, and never as a medical claim. The civilizational analysis
(Part VIII) is a diagnostic lens, not prophecy. Nothing in this book
asks for belief; the mathematics invites verification, the practice
invites direct experience, and the register tags tell you at every
moment which invitation you are holding.

## The Wright Clause

One conviction organizes this edition: *if the framework is true, it
should let us build things — and we do not need the completed formalism
before we start building.* The Wright brothers flew in 1903 on
wind-tunnel data and iteration, decades before theoretical aerodynamics
matured; thermodynamics was reverse-engineered from engines that already
worked. In that spirit, chapters end with **What This Lets Us Build**:
the device, experiment, kit, or practice the chapter's ideas imply. The
wind tunnel's modern descendant is the blinded, pre-registered test
(Chapter 30) — it is how a hypothesis earns the right to be called an
engine, and its stopping rules are how a wrong hypothesis earns an
honorable grave.
