# PART VI: The Physics of Death, Memory, and Reincarnation (v2 — relabel notes)

*All three chapters are register-[I] readings framed in the vocabulary of
Parts I–IV. They carry over with tags and two footnotes.*

- **Ch. 15, Death as a Coherence Filter [I]:** the filter image (what
  survives: capacity without content; what does not: names, faces,
  narrative) carries over as the framework's reading of the
  contemplative literature it cites (Stevenson, Tucker — whose case
  collections are themselves contested but real scholarly artifacts;
  the book cites them as phenomenology, not proof).
- **Ch. 16, Why Most People Cannot Recall Past Lives [I]:** carries
  over; its internal logic (topology-specific modes stripped,
  predisposition retained) is consistent with the Part's premises and
  claims nothing beyond them.
- **Ch. 17, The Rainbow Body [I]:** carries over as the framework's
  reading of the Tibetan jalü accounts (Reynolds' Dzogchen translations
  in the bibliography). Footnote for honesty: the described mechanism
  ("controlled de-excitation down the φ-ladder, emitted as light") is
  imagery mapped onto testimony, not physics with a calculation behind
  it — and v2 says so, which costs the story nothing. Wonder does not
  need laundering.

**What This Lets Us Build.** Nothing in this Part is instrumentable, and
v2 resists pretending otherwise. Its function is meaning, and its
register tag protects both the meaning and the physics from each other.
