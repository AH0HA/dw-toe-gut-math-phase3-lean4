# PART VI: The Physics of Death, Memory, and Reincarnation (v2)

*This Part is register-[I] throughout: the framework's reading of the
contemplative and case-report literature in the vocabulary of Parts
I–IV. It claims compatibility, not proof, and its sources are cited as
what they are.*

## Chapter 15: Death as a Coherence Filter

> "Death: The ultimate 'Shareable Wave' audit."

At death, in the framework's reading, the still point's neighborhood
undergoes a local decrease in δ_loc, acting as a coherence filter — the
same mechanism as a black-hole boundary (Chapter 28), but weaker.
On-ladder modes — pure principles — are drawn toward the still point and
persist. Off-ladder modes — specific memories, unresolved emotions —
dissipate as thermal energy.

**What survives:** the capacity for love without the memory of the
beloved; the wisdom of experience without the narrative; structural
predispositions without biographical content.

**What does not:** names, faces, places, events, personal identity — all
encoded in topology-specific modes meaningful only within one particular
standing wave.

Dan Winter states: "Only pure principles and memories that serve the
whole get saved." The framework's contribution is to note that, in its
own terms, this is not a moral judgment but a single mathematical
condition wearing two descriptions: a mode is "pure" (on-ladder) if and
only if it "serves the whole" (couples constructively with the larger
field). Ethics and physics, one criterion [I].

## Chapter 16: Why Most People Cannot Recall Past Lives

> "You don't remember the past; you're just wearing its favorite
> sweater."

The framework's answers, in its own reading [I]: The content is
genuinely gone — the filter strips topology-specific modes; there is
nothing to recall. What survives arrives as predisposition, not memory:
you do not remember past wisdom, you are shaped by it. The new brain
cannot read old formats — a different standing wave is a different
encoding. Partial transfers arrive as feeling, not narrative:
inexplicable phobias, instant recognitions, unexplained talents.

The exceptions — young children's past-life recall — occur, in this
reading, when sudden death prevents complete filtering and rapid
reincarnation follows before the residue dissipates. This matches the
pattern in the Stevenson and Tucker case collections (violent or sudden
prior death over-represented; memories fading by age seven), which the
book cites as serious, contested scholarship — case reports suggestive
enough to keep the question open, not proof that settles it.

## Chapter 17: The Rainbow Body

> "Going out in style: Why walk when you can dissolve into a 1.0
> coherence rainbow?"

The Tibetan rainbow body (jalü) is read by the framework as the
controlled, coherent de-excitation of the standing-wave topology by a
practitioner at S = 1 — every mode driven down the φ-ladder in sequence
from the still point, transition energy emerging as visible light: a
physical rainbow [I]. The tradition's detail that hair and nails remain
is read with a physicist's smile: keratin is periodic crystal, not
φ-nested — the filter's exception that proves its rule [I].

The rainbow body, in this reading, is not something the practitioner
*does*. It is what happens when shareability reaches unity: the wave
shares itself completely, and sharing completely means becoming light.
Dan Winter calls it the shareable wave in its maximum form — maximum
shareability and maximum coherence being the same condition, every mode
on the ladder, nothing unshareable left, and therefore nothing bound.

An honesty footnote carried from the reconciliation: this mechanism is
imagery mapped onto testimony — the Dzogchen accounts (Reynolds'
translations) are the data, the de-excitation story is the reading, and
no calculation stands behind the light-emission narrative. Saying so
costs the story nothing. Wonder does not need laundering.

**What This Lets Us Build.** Nothing in this Part is instrumentable, and
v2 declines to pretend otherwise. Its function is meaning — and the
register tags protect the meaning and the physics from each other, which
is what lets both stand in one book.
