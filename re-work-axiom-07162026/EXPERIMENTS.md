# From Framework to Bench: Honest Experimental Designs (2026-07-16)

The framework's owner intends to build devices and experiments from it,
and reports strong personal experience with existing φ-technology
(phi-ray, Thera-phi). This document turns that intent into designs that
can produce evidence — the kind that forces attention rather than
inviting dismissal.

## The one principle that matters

Personal experience establishes that *something* happens. It cannot, by
itself, establish *why* — expectation, relaxation, ritual, attention, and
genuine field effects all produce real felt changes. The framework's
distinctive claim is not "the device does something"; it is that
**golden-ratio structure specifically** is what does it. So every design
below isolates exactly that variable: φ-structured stimulus vs. matched
non-φ control, with participants and assessors blinded. A blinded φ-vs-
control win is publishable evidence no reviewer can wave away, and it
validates the engineering claim independently of whether the E₈ story is
true.

An encouraging calibration point: Phase 1 of the book's Ch. 34 practice —
0.1 Hz breathing — is already mainstream-validated. Six breaths per
minute is the standard resonance-frequency HRV-biofeedback protocol
(baroreflex resonance; Lehrer et al.). Part of the practice stack works
for known physiological reasons. The open question is whether φ-spacing
adds anything *beyond* that — which is precisely what blinding can
answer.

## Design 1 — The φ-discriminant device test (Thera-phi / phi-ray)

*The most direct test of the framework's distinctive claim, using
hardware the owner already trusts.*

- One device, two firmware programs: (A) the standard φ-ratio frequency
  cascade; (B) a control cascade matched in power, band, session length,
  and perceived character, but with ratios scrambled (e.g., rational or
  randomized spacing). A/B assignment randomized per session,
  double-blind (neither participant nor operator knows which runs).
- Pre-registered endpoints, chosen *before* data collection: HRV metrics
  (RMSSD, LF power at 0.1 Hz), EEG band ratios, plus subjective scales.
  Pre-registration is what separates a finding from a fishing trip.
- Analysis: within-subject paired comparison, n ≥ 20 sessions per arm.
- Outcomes: φ > control under blinding → a genuine, publishable anomaly
  and the framework's first hard evidence. φ = control → the devices
  still "work," but via mechanisms (relaxation, expectation, entrainment
  at any spacing) that don't need the framework. Both outcomes are
  valuable; only one is flattering. Run it anyway.

## Design 2 — φ-Resonator Q-factor test (Ch. 30's proposal, made precise)

The framework predicts φ-ladder mode spacing minimizes destructive
intermodulation ("non-destructive interference"). This is a bench
measurement with no human in the loop:

- Build matched resonator networks (acoustic cavities or LC chains) with
  mode ladders spaced by (a) φ, (b) rational ratios (e.g., 3/2), (c) the
  plastic number ρ ≈ 1.3247 — the *other* morphic number
  (see `AXIOMS_V2.md`, Axiom 2).
- Drive with broadband noise; measure Q factors, intermodulation
  products, and energy retention time.
- The ρ arm is the theoretically sharp control: if closure is what
  matters, ρ should share some advantage; if *nearest-rung* closure is
  what matters, φ should stand alone. Either result refines Axiom 2's
  clause (b) empirically.

## Design 3 — Quasicrystal coherence

Icosahedral quasicrystals are real materials (AlPdMn and others), and
photonic quasicrystals have a genuine literature. The framework predicts
anomalous coherence/transport properties tied to φ-scaling. A concrete
entry point: compare photonic bandgap and mode-localization behavior in
icosahedral vs. periodic photonic structures of matched density —
partially already in the literature, so the first step is a literature
review to find where the framework's predictions differ from standard
photonic-crystal theory. Where they don't differ, the framework inherits
the confirmation but predicts nothing new; where they differ is exactly
the experiment worth funding.

## Design 4 — EEG φ-ratio coherence (Ch. 30, with a warning)

Searching EEG spectra for φ-ratios *post hoc* will always succeed —
continuous spectra offer infinitely many ratio pairs (look-elsewhere
effect, the same failure mode as numerology). The honest version is
predictive: specify in advance which band pairs should show φ-locked
phase coupling during the Ch. 34 practice and not during matched
non-practice states, then test on fresh recordings not used to form the
hypothesis.

## What "the framework is true" would look like, operationally

The engineering-from-hypothesis stance is legitimate — much of technology
preceded its theory (steam engines before thermodynamics; in fact
thermodynamics was reverse-engineered *from* the working engines). The
program: treat "φ-recursive structure maximizes coherence" as the
operating hypothesis, build devices that should exploit it, and let
blinded measurements decide. If Designs 1–2 produce φ-specific effects,
the framework has earned experiments at the next scale regardless of
what happens to any particular equation. If they don't, the devices
remain useful for the reasons mainstream physiology already understands —
and the framework's claims retreat to the interpretive register where
they can live honestly.
