*   Symptom 1: Mode Decoupling[cite: 1]
*   Symptom 2: Increasing Normal-Fluid Fraction[cite: 1]
*   Symptom 3: Harmonic Beating[cite: 1]

## Chapter 21: Fragmentation vs. Dissolution[cite: 1]
*   Orderly Fragmentation: The wave breaks into smaller but internally coherent pieces.[cite: 1]
*   Chaotic Dissolution: The wave shatters into incoherent noise. Almost no structured fragments survive.[cite: 1]

## Chapter 22: The Prescription at Every Scale[cite: 1]
"If you can't grow it or fix it, the Reset won't let you keep it."[cite: 1]
For an Individual: Maintain coherence between mind and body (practical skills, embodied practices, real relationships).[cite: 1]
For a Family: Shared practice and internal coherence.[cite: 1]
For a Country: Food sovereignty, distributed governance, domestic manufacturing, practical education.[cite: 1]
For Humanity: Strengthen the connections between global knowledge and local practice.[cite: 1]
